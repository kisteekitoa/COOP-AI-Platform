# COOP-AI Business Architecture

COOP-AI is designed to support the business operations of a modern Islamic cooperative through structured workflows, transparent governance, and AI-assisted decision support.

## 1. Business Vision

The vision of COOP-AI is to create a modern Islamic cooperative environment where members receive faster service, management gains better visibility, and operations remain transparent, accountable, and efficient. The platform should support business growth while preserving trust, governance, and compliance.

## 2. Business Objectives

The business objectives of COOP-AI include:

- Better member services
- Faster financing approval
- Transparent operations
- Paperless workflow
- Better management decisions
- Regulatory compliance
- Sustainable growth

## 4. Core Business Processes

COOP-AI supports end-to-end cooperative workflows across the core business functions.

### Member Registration
Members are registered, verified, and assigned appropriate accounts and permissions. The process includes identity validation, profile creation, and onboarding steps.

### Savings
The savings process covers opening an account, processing deposits and withdrawals, maintaining balances, and producing status updates for members and management.

### Financing Application
Members submit financing requests through a structured process that captures eligibility, purpose, requested amount, and supporting documentation.

### Credit Analysis
The credit analysis process reviews applicant information, financial behavior, risk factors, and supporting evidence before recommendation.

### Approval Workflow
Approvals are routed through the appropriate authority levels, including operational review, credit review, and final approval.

### Disbursement
Approved financing is released according to approved terms, with tracking of status and supporting records.

### Repayment
Repayment schedules are monitored, payments are recorded, and overdue balances are flagged for follow-up.

### Accounting Posting
Financial transactions are posted to appropriate accounting records to ensure consistency, traceability, and reporting integrity.

### Reporting
Operational and executive reporting provides visibility into member activity, financing performance, savings growth, and overall business health.

## 5. Business Actors

The business architecture includes the following primary actors:

- Member: Uses cooperative services and interacts with the system for account and request-related activities.
- Officer: Supports member onboarding, service delivery, and operational coordination.
- Branch Manager: Oversees branch operations, approvals, and service quality.
- Credit Committee: Reviews and approves financing decisions based on business and risk criteria.
- Finance Officer: Oversees financial operations, transactions, and reporting support.
- Accountant: Maintains financial records and controls accounting processes.
- Executive: Reviews strategic performance, dashboards, and business outcomes.
- Administrator: Configures and governs system operations and user access.
- Auditor: Reviews business activity, audit trail, and compliance evidence.
